SeedCore Engine — Third-Party Asset Notices
==========================================

This file lists third-party assets whose data is embedded (in whole, in part, or
as a derived form) in engine content shipped with a build. Ship this file, or an
equivalent credits screen entry, alongside the executable.


Parametric Animal presets  (GraphicsEngine/Avatar/Preset/SeedAnimal.ac)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The animal body-type shapes are derived (retargeted / morphed onto a shared
topology) from the following source meshes:

  "Animated Animal Pack" by Quaternius
    https://quaternius.com/  /  https://poly.pizza/bundle/Animated-Animal-Pack-ILAPXeUYiS
    License: CC0 1.0 (public domain) — no attribution required, listed for reference.

  "Cougar" by Poly by Google
    https://poly.pizza/m/8nuCN-gn2s-
    License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
    Modified: retargeted to a shared low-poly topology and blended into a
    parametric shape axis.
